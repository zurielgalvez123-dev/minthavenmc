10. Where crate keys are not sold

Crate keys are a randomised-reward item. Paid randomised rewards are treated as gambling in some places, so crate key packages are not offered to buyers in Belgium, or in any other country where paid randomised in-game rewards are prohibited. Ranks and claim blocks are fixed contents and are unaffected.

Crate keys can also be earned in-game without paying — through voting, playtime and events — and the published odds for every crate are the same whether the key was bought or earned.
